# Business Understanding & Project Motivation

## Target Audience & Problem Statement
International students arriving in Germany often struggle with the strict, highly structured German waste separation system. Incorrect disposal leads to environmental contamination and potential monetary fines from local authorities or housing managers.

## German Waste Categories Mapping
Our automated classification system targets the primary German recycling streams:

1. *Gelber Sack / Gelbe Tonne (Yellow Sack):* Lightweight packaging materials, plastics, metals, composite materials (TetraPak), and tin cans.
2. *Restmüll (Residual Waste):* Non-recyclable household items, dirty packaging, sanitary products, and heavily soiled items.
3. *Papier / Pappe (Paper & Cardboard):* Clean paper, cardboard boxes, newspapers, and magazines.
4. *Bioabfall (Organic Waste):* Food leftovers, compostable organic matter, and garden waste.
5. *Altglas (Glass):* Glass bottles and jars (divided by white, green, and brown glass when applicable).

## Expected Impact
Provide an accessible computer vision tool that helps international residents classify waste items accurately using standard smartphone photography in uncontrolled environments.
